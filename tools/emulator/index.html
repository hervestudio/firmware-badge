BADGE EMULATOR

A pixel-perfect, in-browser emulator of the threejs.paris conference badge — the real ESP32 firmware compiled to WebAssembly.

←/↑ previous  ·  →/↓ next  ·  space center button (tap = menu/select, hold 2 s = power off)
In action games: center = act · quit = hold ←+→ for 0.8 s · game over: next = play again
Animations & games are the real firmware sources (anims_extra.h, games.h, tama.h) compiled to WebAssembly.
